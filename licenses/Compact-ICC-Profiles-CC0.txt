Compact-ICC-Profiles: AdobeCompat-v2.icc and ProPhoto-v4.icc
============================================================

assets/AdobeCompat-v2.icc and assets/ProPhoto-v4.icc are copied unchanged from
Clinton Ingram's Compact-ICC-Profiles, https://github.com/saucecontrol/Compact-ICC-Profiles,
at commit bdd84663061bc4ae95ca70decff54f581e27f702 (profiles/AdobeCompat-v2.icc and
profiles/ProPhoto-v4.icc). They are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0,
whose text, from that commit's `license` file, follows.

SHA-256:
60fb2adecacf82132db0b1c09b303316f3bbd9e2823e7ba096d01627d12d57c9  AdobeCompat-v2.icc
090daf740c136b4a63bf979d64f034b4a65aa5abbb04a0917729222afe2bb5c2  ProPhoto-v4.icc

Neither is an Adobe file. The project's readme says the colorants and white points
of its Adobe-compatible profiles were adapted from the published x,y chromaticities
of those spaces (Adobe RGB (1998) here), and describes ProPhoto-v4.icc as ROMM RGB
(ISO 22028-2) with a parametric curve. RAWmakase's tests check both profiles against
its own conversion (crates/rawmakase-decode/src/color_management.rs).
