RAWmakase 0.7.2 makes **export faster on computers with many CPUs** and safer: it now defaults to the Desktop, and it never replaces another photo's original. **Manage Presets** and **Manage Profiles** let you tidy your lists, presets show as thumbnails of your photo, and collections can be driven from MCP and `rawmakase control`.

Crop corners and sides stay put when the aspect is locked, a mask's overlay gets out of the way while you move its sliders, and Library previews now follow your monitor profile.

## New

- **Presets as thumbnails of the open photo.** The Presets panel shows each preset applied to the photo you're editing, so you can pick by look. ([#1244](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1244), [#804](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/804))
- **Manage Presets.** The **+** in the Presets panel header opens a window where you reorder groups by dragging, hide presets or whole groups (built-in ones too), rename groups, delete or export your own presets (a preset as `.xmp`, a group as `.zip`) and drag a preset to another of your groups. Import stays in the same window. ([#1217](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1217), by [@m-stanczak](https://github.com/m-stanczak))
- **Manage Profiles.** **Manage Profiles…** in the Develop Profile menu (and in Preferences under Camera profiles) lists every profile and LUT in your library. Rename a look, move it to another group or rename a group, hide groups from the menu, star favorites, or move a file to the Trash. Adobe's own looks and DCPs keep their names. ([#1209](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1209), [#1206](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1206))
- **Export in a photo's right-click menu.** ([#1230](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1230), [#1228](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1228))
- **Collections from MCP and `rawmakase control`.** List, create (including collection sets), fill and empty collections, and find photos by capture date. Each change is one Undo step, the open Library follows it and no file on disk is touched. ([#1208](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1208), [#1207](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1207))
- **Looks with Red, Green and Blue point curves import.** Looks such as RNI's Kodak Portra 160 used to fail with "Unsupported profile channel curve". They now import and render as Camera Raw 18.7 does, to within about 0.4 ΔE00 on the test chart (0.5 to 0.8 with a master S curve). ([#1214](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1214), [#949](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/949))

## Improved

- **Export uses more of a many-core computer.** Several photos export at once, one per 6 logical CPUs (rounded, up to 8), and pixel work now uses every logical CPU instead of at most 8. Computers with 8 or fewer logical CPUs still export one photo at a time. On a 16-thread Ryzen 7 8745HS exporting 16 Sony ARWs (24 MP) to full-size JPEG, the time fell from about 45 s to about 33 s, at about 4 GB of peak memory instead of 1.6 GB. Exports run at background priority, so Develop comes first. Files now land in the folder out of the photos' order. ([#1243](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1243), [#1232](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1232), [#1229](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1229))
- **Locked crop corners and sides stay put.** With the aspect locked (the default), dragging a corner keeps the opposite corner where it is, and dragging a side outward grows the crop, as in Lightroom. ([#1247](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1247))
- **A mask's overlay hides while you move its sliders**, so you can see what they do. **O** or **Show Overlay** brings it back. ([#1247](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1247))
- **Space opens the Loupe and toggles the zoom**, as in Lightroom. ([#1246](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1246))
- **The brush stroke shows while you paint it.** ([#1234](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1234))
- **Library previews follow your monitor profile**, as Develop already did. Before, the grid, filmstrip, Loupe, Compare and Survey showed sRGB as device color, so a photo could look different from Develop. ([#1231](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1231))
- **Controls have names for screen readers.** Every view was audited for unnamed controls. ([#1235](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1235))
- **Fit Camera Look works on Google's HDR+ DNGs** (Pixel phones, and Google Camera ports). They failed with "The preview's colour space is not stated". Their previews now read as sRGB. Some Pixel models still can't be fitted. ([#1213](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1213), [#1211](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1211))

## Fixed

- **Export no longer replaces another photo's original.** With Overwrite, a name template could give a photo the file name of another photo, such as the camera JPEG beside a raw, and replace it. Every photo of the batch is now protected, and the photo fails with "Cannot overwrite source …". ([#1245](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1245))
- **Unique names after a clash now count on from the base name** (`a-3`, not `a-2-2`). ([#1245](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1245))
- **A failure in the export queue's own bookkeeping no longer stops later exports.** Such a batch is now reported as failed and the queue goes on. ([#1236](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1236))
- **Shift+Cmd+V (Paste Settings) and Alt+Cmd+V work when the clipboard has no text**, for example after a screenshot. On Windows and Linux the same goes for Ctrl+Shift+V and Ctrl+Alt+V. ([#1241](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1241), with a fix by [@m-stanczak](https://github.com/m-stanczak))
- **A preset without an Amount keeps the Amount's place.** ([#1238](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1238), by [@m-stanczak](https://github.com/m-stanczak))
- **A double click on a title-bar button no longer zooms the window.** ([#1237](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1237), by [@m-stanczak](https://github.com/m-stanczak))

## Changed

- **A first export goes to the Desktop**, not beside the originals. A destination you chose before is kept. ([#1248](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1248))

## Upgrade notes

- Saved edits render as before. The Library's thumbnails and previews may look slightly different if you use a monitor profile, because they now go through it.

## Known limitations

- Several photos export at once only on computers with more than 8 logical CPUs. The speedup was measured on one 16-thread computer, not on a 32-thread one.
- Fit Camera Look still can't fit some Pixel models (3a, 4a, 6 Pro), whose preview doesn't line up with the render.
