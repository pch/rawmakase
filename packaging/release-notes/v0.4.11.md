RAWmakase 0.4.11 adds AI Denoise, which removes noise from high-ISO Bayer and X-Trans RAWs with a neural network that runs on your computer, and offers free community camera profiles for 72 camera bodies straight from Develop. Presets made in Camera Raw 18 import more cleanly, Sony and Pentax photos are framed where their JPEGs and Camera Raw frame them, and older Nikon lenses are recognised from their focal range.

## New

- **AI Denoise.** **Detail ▸ AI Denoise** has a switch and an Amount (0–100, default 50, as in Lightroom) for Bayer and X-Trans RAWs. It uses RawNIND UtNet2 by Benoit Brummer (UCLouvain, CC BY 4.0), which denoises and demosaics the photo in one pass, entirely on this computer. The first use asks you to accept the model's licence and download it (31 MB), and it can be removed from the same place. The result is computed once per photo, cached on disk (its size, location and purge are in Preferences), and blended by Amount before manual noise reduction, so Amount changes are quick. The part of the photo in view at 100% is shown first. Switching it on sets manual Luminance and Color noise reduction to 0. AI Denoise travels in XMP sidecars and Copy/Sync (as its own Detail group, never onto rendered files), but not in presets. A photo's XMP sidecar with Lightroom's Denoise imports as AI Denoise at its Amount. An export or `rawmakase render` with AI Denoise on gets the denoised image or fails that photo with the reason, never the plain image. With AI Denoise off, nothing renders differently. ([#799](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/799), [#737](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/737), [#753](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/753))
- **Community camera profiles.** When a RAW from a covered camera is open in Develop and no Adobe profile is imported or available for it, a **Community** offer below the **Profile** menu suggests a free profile made for that camera, with its source, credit and terms. **Download & Apply** fetches that exact file, checks its size and SHA-256, adds it to your profile library and applies it as one History step; an installed profile offers **Apply** without a download. **Profile ▸ Show community profiles for this camera** brings a dismissed offer back. The catalogue has 74 public-domain or CC0 profiles from RawTherapee and ART, covering 72 bodies from Nikon, Canon, Fujifilm, Sony, Panasonic, Olympus, Pentax and Samsung. Matching happens on your computer; only an explicit download contacts the profile's source. The per-camera page in Preferences is gone; its import, storage and Lensfun settings remain. ([#762](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/762), [#763](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/763))

## Improved

- **Older Nikon lenses are recognised from their focal range.** Nikon bodies that write no lens name, such as the D3100, share lens numbers between unrelated lenses. RAWmakase now compares the focal range and apertures in the maker note with Adobe's lens profiles, so it picks the right Adobe profile automatically for 118 of the 138 Nikon lenses under shared numbers. Files that still name no lens show Camera Raw's name, such as "18.0-55.0 mm f/3.5-5.6", and Lensfun offers the lenses that fit instead of "Lens not named". ([#792](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/792))
- **Shadows and Highlights follow Camera Raw more closely on photos with small bright spots.** The global Shadows/Highlights rule now sees how sparse the top of the histogram is, so a tiny clipped highlight no longer makes the rest of the photo look darker than Camera Raw treats it. Across the validation sets, 34 non-default renders came closer to Camera Raw and 20 moved further away; default renders are unchanged. ([#782](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/782))
- **Page Up and Page Down in the Library grid.** They move the active photo by a screen of rows, and Shift extends the selection. ([#777](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/777))
- **Crisper interface icons.** Icons are drawn on whole pixels, so they are as sharp as the text beside them at every interface scale. ([#784](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/784), [#783](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/783))

## Fixed

- **Camera Raw 18 presets with an inactive Enhance filter work.** Presets and sidecars whose Enhance filter has everything off no longer show as unsupported with "Unsupported structured setting: FilterList". Active Enhance settings (Denoise, Raw Details, Super Resolution) are named instead, and the rest of the preset can still be applied. ([#795](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/795), [#789](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/789))
- **Preset packs import their look profiles.** Importing presets now also imports the pack's look profiles beside them, so its presets work at once instead of reporting "Missing or unsupported enhanced profile". Onboarding's Presets row also picks up the third-party looks Lightroom installed. ([#796](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/796), [#790](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/790))
- **Sony and Pentax photos are framed correctly.** On the a7R III, III A, IV and IV A, the a7 III and a7 IV in APS-C modes, and the Pentax K-3 Mark III, K-70 and KF, the default frame was 8 to 36 sensor pixels off; it now matches the camera's JPEG and Camera Raw. ([#779](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/779))
- **10- and 12-bit 4:2:0 AVIF photos show the right colours on ARM.** On Apple Silicon, Windows on ARM and ARM Linux, every other pixel's colour was wrong. ([#794](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/794))
- **JPEG exports from photos with large EXIF are no longer corrupt.** A source with more than about 64 KB of EXIF produced a broken JPEG that was reported as exported. The EXIF is now trimmed to fit, dropping the largest capture values first and keeping make, model, capture time, lens and GPS. ([#775](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/775), [#774](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/774))
- **Thumbnails stay after the Import Adobe profiles shortcuts.** The Inspector's **Import Adobe profiles for this camera** and **Import Adobe base profile** no longer drop the open photo's thumbnail when its edit did not change. ([#781](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/781), [#766](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/766))
- **Control and MCP requests work while the window is hidden.** Requests no longer expire as `cancelled`, and photos opened through control finish loading, when the window is behind other apps or minimized. ([#797](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/797), [#791](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/791))

## Upgrade notes

- On the Sony and Pentax bodies above, crops and masks saved in RAWmakase move by 8 to 36 sensor pixels with the corrected frame. Crops from Lightroom now land where Lightroom put them.
- The first time a catalogue stores an AI Denoise setting (switched on, or its Amount moved), it is upgraded to version 5, after writing a backup beside it. Earlier releases refuse a version 5 catalogue and edits saved with AI Denoise settings, so close it on other computers first and update them too. Catalogues without AI Denoise settings are unchanged.

## Known limitations

- AI Denoise runs on the GPU only on Apple Silicon (through CoreML). Elsewhere it uses the CPU: on an M1 Pro, a 24 MP photo took 3.4 s with CoreML and 20 s on the CPU. GPU support for Linux and Windows is tracked in [#798](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/798).
- AI Denoise results are computed when a photo is opened or exported, not ahead of time for synced photos, and Library thumbnails don't show them.
- Compared with Camera Raw's Denoise on 100% crops, results are close at ISO 12,800–20,000 but behind at ISO 32,000 and above (colour blotches in dark areas, less detail) and softer on X-Trans. Lightroom's Denoise is imported from XMP sidecars, not yet from Lightroom catalogues.
- Community profiles are alternatives made by other projects, not a claim of Adobe parity. Dismissing an offer lasts until RAWmakase quits.
