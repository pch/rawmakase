RAWmakase 0.6.1 frames photos with an imported Adobe lens profile as Lightroom does, redesigns the controls in Develop's panels and keeps catalogs from growing with leftovers.

Wide-angle photos corrected with an Adobe lens profile used to come out about 3% tighter than in Lightroom and Camera Raw, and close-focus Canon CR2 photos took the profile's infinity entry. Both now match Lightroom's framing much more closely. Catalogs no longer carry a copy of the Lightroom catalog they were imported from, unused mask and AI Remove pixels, or every old upgrade backup. Also in this release: Lightroom 5 edits keep their tone curve, Library previews rebuild less often, the interface uses the system font on macOS and Windows, and a possible fix for screen flicker on Windows.

## Improved

- **Imported Adobe lens profiles frame the photo as Lightroom does.** Lightroom and Camera Raw zoom in or out until the corrected edge touches the crop. RAWmakase only zoomed in, so a barrel correction at the wide end of a zoom came out about 3% tighter (1 / 0.97 on a Canon EF 24-105mm f/4L at 24mm). On 5 of 6 such photos, RAWmakase now matches Lightroom's framing to within 0.05%. Built-in, DNG and Lensfun corrections frame as before. ([#1136](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1136))
- **Canon CR2 photos use the lens profile entry for their focus distance.** Many Adobe profiles have entries for several focus distances, and RAWmakase always took the farthest. A 50 mm photo focused at about 0.5 m came out 0.45% tighter than in Lightroom, and now differs by 0.13%. CR3 and other makes record no distance yet and keep the farthest entry. ([#1137](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1137))
- **Develop's panels are redesigned.** Sliders have their label and value above a full-width track and a larger knob, and the value box edits as a filled field. Panel contents line up on one inset. In Basic, Black & White and HDR are switches in one row, and Auto sits on the Tone heading. Drop-downs are taller and outlined. The Tone Curve has one toolbar with its channels as coloured dots, a histogram tinted per channel, and an Input/Output row that no longer moves the panel when you select a point. ([#1130](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1130))
- **Catalogs stay smaller.**
  - Importing a Lightroom catalog no longer stores a copy of the `.lrcat` inside the RAWmakase catalog. Catalogs that already carry one shrink once when this release opens them. ([#1114](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1114))
  - Mask rasters and AI Remove patches that no edit, History step, snapshot or virtual copy refers to are deleted, after nothing has used them for a week. This check runs at most once a week. On one real catalog this took it from 189 to 155 MiB. ([#1132](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1132), [#1112](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1112))
  - Only the newest upgrade backup (`<name>.before-upgrade-backup`) is kept beside a catalog. Older ones are deleted the next time the catalog opens after an upgrade. Renamed backups are left alone. ([#1131](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1131), [#1113](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1113))
- **Library previews rebuild less often.** A RAW's thumbnail from its embedded preview is now kept when the rendering engine changes, so it isn't read from the file again after an update, which was slow over SMB. Editing a photo no longer leaves a thumbnail for every saved edit. When the cache is full, it deletes thumbnails that can no longer be shown first. ([#1115](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1115))
- **The interface uses the system font on macOS and Windows** (San Francisco and Segoe UI). Linux keeps Inter. Text watermarks default to the system font. ([#1119](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1119))
- **`rawmakase render` and `render-batch` take `--quality`** (1–100, default 92) for JPEG output, including gain-map HDR JPEGs. ([#1117](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1117))

## Fixed

- **Lightroom 5 edits from before process version 2012 keep their tone curve.** Such records can carry both the old and the 2012 point curve. RAWmakase took the 2012 one, usually a linear default, so these photos lost their Medium Contrast or custom curve, including the Red, Green and Blue curves. The record's process version now picks the curve, as in Lightroom, for catalog imports and XMP sidecars. ([#1127](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1127), [#1079](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1079))
- **Possible fix for screen flicker on Windows.** On one Windows 10 PC with an NVIDIA card and an SDR monitor, the screen flickered every few seconds while RAWmakase was open. RAWmakase now asks the graphics driver about HDR only on displays that Windows reports as HDR-capable. We couldn't reproduce the flicker, so this fix is unconfirmed. Please tell us in [#1126](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1126) if you still see it. ([#1134](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1134))
- **A value that rounds to zero no longer shows as "-0".** ([#1130](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1130))

## Upgrade notes

- **Edits that use an imported Adobe lens profile render wider**, as in Lightroom. A crop or mask made on the tighter framing shifts with the photo.
- **The first open of a catalog that holds an archived Lightroom catalog compacts it**, shown as "Making the catalog smaller…". This needs free disk space up to the catalog's size. If it fails, the catalog opens anyway and the next open tries again.
- **Lightroom data RAWmakase never read stays only in the `.lrcat`**: faces, smart collection rules, publish services, and the GPS and IPTC fields RAWmakase doesn't copy. Photos, develop settings, History, snapshots, collections, stacks, keywords and metadata already imported are kept.
- **After this release opens a catalog, export it as a catalog with this release.** Earlier releases' **Export as Catalog** refuses it because of a new table. They still open the catalog as before. ([#1138](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1138))
- **On macOS and Windows, text watermark presets that name Inter** need another font, since Inter is no longer bundled there.
- The Lightroom 5 tone curve fix applies to Lightroom edits RAWmakase reads from now on. Photos already in your catalog keep the edit they were imported with.
