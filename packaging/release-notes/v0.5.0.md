RAWmakase 0.5.0 brings two big additions: extensions and HDR editing.

**Extensions** add commands, settings, panels, mask generators and control devices to RAWmakase without changing the app. MCP, MIDI and AI Masks (Select Subject, Sky and Background) are now bundled extensions, which you can turn off in **Preferences ▸ Extensions**. You can install other extensions from a file or a GitHub repository.

**HDR editing** lets you turn on High Dynamic Range in Develop, as in Lightroom and Camera Raw. You can see light above SDR white on an HDR display and export it as HDR TIFF, JPEG XL or a gain-map JPEG. Lightroom's HDR edits open with their HDR settings, and HDR files (PQ and HLG, and gain-map JPEG, JPEG XL and HEIC) open as HDR edits. **HDR isn't finished yet.** Some controls don't render in HDR, and on Windows and Linux HDR display hasn't been checked on a real HDR screen. See [Known limitations](#known-limitations).

Also in this release: shortcuts you can rebind, a Library metadata filter, Lightroom panorama DNGs, much faster work from network volumes, and fixes for hard-linked files, ntfs-3g drives and Synchronize Folder on Windows.

## New

- **Extensions.** An extension is a package with a `rawmakase-extension.toml` manifest and usually a program that runs as its own process. It can add:
  - commands, which you can run from menus, the command palette, a shortcut or `rawmakase control action`. A command can ask you to click points or draw a box on the photo first;
  - settings and panels drawn by the app;
  - mask generators that work like Select Subject;
  - control adapters, such as a MIDI device or an MCP server.

  **Preferences ▸ Extensions** lists the extensions you have, with what each one says it can reach (the network, files, devices), and lets you enable, disable, update and remove them. **Install from GitHub…** and **Install from File…** add new ones, which are marked **Not reviewed**. An extension runs with your permissions, like a Blender add-on, so only install ones you trust. Installing never runs an extension's code; its program starts the first time you use one of its features. `rawmakase extensions` does the same from the command line. Developers write extensions in Rust with the `rawmakase-extension` SDK and package them with `cargo rawmakase`. See the [extensions docs](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/blob/v0.5.0/docs/extensions/overview.md). ([#1011](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1011), [#1012](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1012), [#1021](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1021), [#1026](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1026), [#1038](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1038), [#1039](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1039), [#1045](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1045))
- **MCP, MIDI and AI Masks are bundled extensions.** They come with the app, are on by default, update with it, and can be turned off but not removed. When MCP is off, `rawmakase mcp` stops and refuses to start. MIDI's device page is now under **Preferences ▸ Extensions ▸ MIDI**, and it keeps your `midi.json`. Select Subject, Sky and Background run through AI Masks and keep the models you have already downloaded. ([#1013](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1013), [#1014](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1014), [#1040](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1040), [#1041](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1041))
- **HDR editing.** Turn on **High Dynamic Range** in the Basic panel to edit a photo in HDR. On an HDR display, Develop shows light above SDR white in Fit, at 100%, in Before/After and in Reference View. On macOS this uses an EDR display. On Windows it uses Advanced Color, and on Linux under Wayland the colour-management protocol. While you drag sliders, the GPU renders the preview. The histogram and RGB readout show stops above SDR white and mark the light your display can't show. **Preview for SDR display** shows what SDR screens and outputs will get. Each edit has two versions. The HDR version is measured against Camera Raw 18.7's HDR output. The SDR version is the same edit with HDR off, and the Library, thumbnails, SDR exports and SDR displays use it. ([#253](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/253), [#905](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/905), [#907](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/907), [#915](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/915), [#924](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/924), [#960](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/960), [#975](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/975), [#983](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/983), [#985](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/985), [#994](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/994))
- **Lightroom and Camera Raw HDR edits carry over.** Lightroom's HDR settings (`HDREditMode`, `HDRMaxValue` and the SDR rendition sliders) are read and written in XMP, Camera Raw presets, Lightroom catalog imports, native presets and Copy/Sync. Your HDR edits are kept after you reopen the catalog, including in virtual copies, History and snapshots. ([#905](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/905), [#1033](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1033))
- **HDR export.** In **Export ▸ File Settings**, turn on **HDR** for TIFF, JPEG XL or JPEG:
  - **TIFF:** PQ or HLG at 16-bit, or linear light at 32-bit float;
  - **JPEG XL:** PQ or HLG, lossy or lossless, or with a gain map;
  - **JPEG:** an ISO 21496-1 gain map over the SDR version, which looks right on SDR screens too.

  Each comes in Rec. 2020, Display P3 or Rec. 709. JPEG XL exports were checked with independent decoders. `rawmakase render --hdr-export` does the same from the command line. ([#894](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/894), [#908](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/908), [#909](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/909), [#911](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/911), [#912](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/912), [#1035](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1035))
- **JPEG XL export.** JPEG XL is a new export format. It can be SDR (8-bit sRGB) or HDR, and lossy (with a **Quality** slider, 92 by default) or **Lossless**. From the command line, use `rawmakase render … out.jxl --jxl-quality N`. ([#906](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/906), [#909](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/909), [#925](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/925), [#1030](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1030))
- **HDR files open as HDR edits.** These files open in Develop with HDR on:
  - PQ, HLG and linear-light TIFF, PNG, JPEG XL and AVIF files;
  - Photoshop's 32-bit float TIFFs;
  - JPEG and JPEG XL files with an ISO 21496-1 gain map, and JPEGs with Adobe's earlier `hdrgm` gain map;
  - PQ and HLG HEIC files, on macOS and on Linux with libheif;
  - iPhone HEIC photos with a gain map, on macOS.

  Their Library previews no longer clip or come out dark. ([#917](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/917), [#922](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/922), [#923](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/923), [#958](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/958), [#962](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/962), [#981](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/981))
- **Rebind keyboard shortcuts.** In **Preferences ▸ Shortcuts**, you can search for an action or a key, click a key to change it, and add a second key to any action. Menus show your keys. ([#989](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/989), [#980](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/980))
- **Filter the Library by metadata.** As in Lightroom's Metadata filter, a strip under the filter bar lets you filter by Camera, Lens, Focal Length, Aperture, Shutter Speed, ISO, File Type and Keyword, with a count for each value. Your choice of columns is saved with the Library layout. ([#948](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/948), [#947](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/947))
- **Lightroom panorama DNGs open.** Panoramas that Lightroom merged and saved as DNGs (lossy JPEG XL tiles) used to fail with "Unsupported RAW variant". They now open, and their empty corners are white, as in Lightroom. ([#953](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/953), [#955](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/955))
- **Thumbnail cache size.** In **Preferences ▸ Performance ▸ Thumbnail cache**, you can choose from 512 MiB (the default) to 16 GiB. A bigger cache means fewer thumbnails are made again from the photos, which helps most on network volumes. ([#919](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/919), [#887](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/887))
- **The Setup Assistant finds Lightroom's synced presets.** It now also finds presets that Lightroom (not Classic) keeps inside its library and shows them under User Presets. ([#928](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/928), [#927](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/927))

## Improved

- **Faster work on network volumes.**
  - Adding a folder uses sixteen readers and finds sidecars in the folder's listing instead of looking for each one. ([#918](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/918), [#885](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/885))
  - Photo info comes from the RAW's header instead of the whole file. ([#920](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/920), [#889](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/889))
  - Develop shows the embedded preview before the whole file has been read. ([#921](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/921))
  - When you switch folders, the thumbnails of the folder you left are skipped. ([#916](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/916), [#888](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/888))
- **Thumbnails and Standard previews match Develop.** Edited thumbnails and Standard previews used to sharpen at about twice the radius, add about 2.4 times the grain and denoise one level off. They now match Develop and exports. ([#951](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/951))
- **Discarding previews makes the cache smaller on disk.** Discard, Clear and Automatically Discard 1:1 Previews used to leave the preview cache file the same size. They now give the space back, a little at a time. ([#956](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/956))
- **The macOS window reopens zoomed** if you quit with it zoomed. ([#933](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/933))

## Fixed

- **Hard-linked photos can be moved to the Trash.** As in Lightroom, only the photo's own name goes to the Trash, and the file stays under its other names. This is still refused when another photo in the catalog is the same file, and the message now names that photo instead of a vague duplicate error. ([#1042](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1042), [#1028](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1028), [#1027](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1027))
- **Photos rename on NTFS drives mounted with ntfs-3g** (Linux Mint, Ubuntu). Before, none of them were renamed. Folders on ntfs-3g still can't be renamed. ([#929](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/929), [#914](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/914))
- **Synchronize Folder adds the new photos it finds** on Windows, where a catalog folder stored without the `\\?\` prefix (as Lightroom imports store it) used to skip them all. The same happened on macOS and Linux when a folder was stored through a symbolic link. ([#944](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/944), [#941](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/941))
- **Library thumbnails no longer flicker** on very large grids with more than 192 thumbnails on screen. ([#904](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/904), [#886](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/886))
- **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Delete moves photos to the Trash on Windows.** Before, the app never saw the shortcut. ([#990](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/990), [#972](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/972))
- **Long shortcuts no longer overlap menu labels** on Windows and Linux. ([#991](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/991), [#979](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/979))
- **The window no longer flashes grey while you resize it on macOS.** ([#969](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/969))
- **A remote worker from another version is refused.** Before, it could store Subject and Sky masks made with its own older processing. Now the selection fails with a message that names both versions. ([#946](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/946), [#945](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/945))
- **Removing the AI Denoise model says "AI Denoise model removed"** instead of "Selection model removed". ([#1016](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1016))

## Upgrade notes

- **Once you save an edit with HDR on, earlier releases can't open the catalog.** Saving an HDR edit raises the catalog format, and RAWmakase 0.4.x refuses to open it rather than misread it. HDR settings saved while HDR is off don't raise the format.
- MIDI is now an extension. On first start it copies your `midi.json` into its own data folder and leaves the original where it was.
- The selection models stay where they were, so Select Subject and Sky don't download them again. If you use a remote worker, update it to 0.5.0 too.
- Settings for Export with Previous saved before this release export JPEG XL losslessly, as before. New settings start lossy.

## Known limitations

**HDR is incomplete.** When an edit uses something HDR can't render yet, the edit keeps HDR on and stays valid, but its HDR version doesn't render. Develop shows the SDR version and the Basic panel says which control is the cause. An HDR export of that edit fails with the same reason. Until RAWmakase matches Camera Raw for these controls, they don't render in HDR:

- **Highlights**, at any value other than 0;
- **Whites** above +50;
- **Clarity** below 0 or above +50, and **Dehaze** below −30;
- the colour mixer's **Luminance** beyond ±40;
- **Defringe**;
- **Refine Saturation** other than 100%;
- a **point curve** whose last point is left of white;
- a darkening post-crop vignette's **Highlights**;
- a mask's **Whites, Blacks, Hue, Saturation, Highlights, Clarity or Dehaze**;
- looks with **split toning**, or that set one of the controls above (Adobe Landscape sets Highlights);
- 27 **HDR camera profiles** with sRGB-encoded tables, and the Fujifilm HS20EXR and HS30EXR profile curves.

Other HDR limitations:

- **HDR display has only been checked on macOS** (a MacBook Pro XDR display). Windows and Linux (Wayland) have HDR display support, but it hasn't been seen on a real HDR screen. Please report how it works for you. **Linux under X11** shows only the SDR version. On Windows and Wayland, HDR shows only if an HDR display is connected when RAWmakase starts.
- **AI Denoise:** an HDR edit with AI Denoise shows its SDR version in Develop. Its HDR export does include AI Denoise.
- **HDR files** (rather than raws) render their HDR preview on the CPU, so slider drags wait for it. This is also true for masks, Point Color, the vignette and grain.
- **HEIC:** HDR HEICs don't open as HDR edits on Windows. HEIC gain maps open as HDR edits on macOS only, and as their SDR image elsewhere.
- **The SDR version differs from Camera Raw's.** RAWmakase's SDR version is the edit with HDR off. Camera Raw's is a local tone map of its HDR version, so the two can differ.
- Lightroom's **Visualize HDR** overlay isn't drawn. The histogram marks the light your display can't show.
- HDR exports can't have a watermark.

Extensions:

- The `rawmakase-extension` SDK and `cargo rawmakase` aren't on crates.io yet. Install `cargo rawmakase` from this repository, as the [publishing guide](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/blob/v0.5.0/docs/extensions/publishing.md) explains.
- RAWmakase shows what an extension says it can reach but doesn't enforce it.
