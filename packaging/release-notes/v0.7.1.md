RAWmakase 0.7.1 adds **Import Photos**, which copies photos from a camera card into your library as Lightroom Classic's Import (Copy) does, and **Merge to HDR in the app**, which now aligns handheld brackets and removes ghosts.

Black & white photos with a colour look or colour grading now render toned, as in Camera Raw, and **V** switches to Adobe Monochrome as Lightroom does. Presets and exported files now carry their looks in a form Lightroom and Camera Raw render. Also in this release: lossy DNGs from phones and Lightroom open, Fit Camera Look matches skin more closely, Remove from Collection is in the thumbnail menu, and there's another fix to try for the screen flicker on Windows.

## New

- **Import Photos from a card.** **Library ▸ Import Photos…** (⌘⇧I, Ctrl+Shift+I on Windows and Linux) copies photos from a camera card, another drive or any folder into your library and adds them to the catalog in one step. It never changes, renames or deletes the originals. ([#1190](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1190), [#806](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/806), [#1205](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1205))
  - **Don't Import Suspected Duplicates** (on by default) greys out photos the catalog already has, matched by original name, capture time and size, even if you've renamed them since.
  - **Rename** with your saved filename templates or Lightroom's *Shoot Name - Original File Number* and *Shoot Name - Sequence*, and choose whether extensions are lowercase, uppercase or left as they are.
  - **Apply During Import** sets Creator and Copyright and adds keywords. They're written to the catalog, so the copies stay byte for byte the same as the files on the card.
  - **Organize** the copies by their original folders, by date (in Lightroom's 13 date formats) or into one folder, optionally inside a subfolder.
  - A preview shows where each photo will go, the folders that will be made and the free space. A name that's already taken gets `-2`, so nothing is overwritten. Each copy is checked against the original. If the app crashes, the card is pulled or you press Stop, the import recovers without losing a finished copy.
- **Merge to HDR in the app.** Select 2 to 9 bracketed RAWs and choose **Photo Merge › HDR…** in the thumbnail or filmstrip menu (also **Photo › Photo Merge › HDR…** in the macOS menu bar). The window lists each photo's exposure and explains why a selection isn't a bracket. The merge produces a floating-point DNG that opens as a raw in RAWmakase, Lightroom and Camera Raw. It's added to the catalog, optionally stacked with its sources, and opened in Develop. ([#1200](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1200), [#1198](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1198), [#1204](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1204))
  - **Auto Align** (on by default) lines up brackets shot handheld. Edges that not every frame covers are cropped off. ([#1194](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1194))
  - **Deghost** (None, Low, Medium or High) takes anything that moved between frames, such as people, branches or waves, from a single frame, so it doesn't show up as a see-through ghost. ([#1196](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1196))
  - The merge starts with the first photo's white balance, profile and lens corrections, as Lightroom's merge does, and the DNG carries an embedded preview. On the command line, `rawmakase merge-hdr` gained `--deghost` and `--catalog … [--stack]`.
- **Remove from Collection in the thumbnail menu.** When you're viewing a collection, right-click a photo (or a selection) to take it out of that collection, or out of the Quick Collection. The photos stay in the catalog and in their other collections. ([#1202](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1202), [#1199](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1199))

## Improved

- **Black & white with a colour look or colour grading renders toned, as in Camera Raw.** A colour look kept under black and white now tints the gray, and Color Grading or split toning colours its shadows, midtones and highlights. Before, these photos rendered plain gray (6 to 16 ΔE00 from Camera Raw on the test chart, now under 0.5). ([#1195](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1195), [#1187](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1187))
- **Black & White (V) switches a colour profile to Adobe Monochrome, as Lightroom Classic does**, when Adobe Monochrome is imported for the camera. Color goes back to the photo's default profile, and Reset on the Basic panel now does the same. Without Adobe Monochrome, the profile stays as before. ([#1191](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1191))
- **Lightroom sidecars and catalog edits take black & white from the look, as Camera Raw does.** A colour look applied over a photo set to black and white renders in colour, and a black & white look over a colour photo renders in black and white. ([#1186](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1186))
- **Fit Camera Look matches the camera's skin tones more closely**, especially on small faces. Measured against the embedded JPEGs of 83 photos with faces, the median skin colour difference fell from 0.92 to 0.69 ΔE00, and the median skin hue error from 0.56° to 0.14°. A look is now fitted for more photos with little colour, such as portraits against grey walls or at night, which used to fall back to a curve only. ([#1197](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1197), [#773](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/773))

## Fixed

- **Looks in presets and exported files now render in Lightroom and Camera Raw.** RAWmakase wrote only a look's name, which Camera Raw rendered as no look and Lightroom applied with no table, so a RAWmakase preset with a look did nothing in Lightroom. Looks are now written with their settings, and imported `.cube` and third-party looks are written with their tables, as Lightroom writes them. Lightroom sidecars that carry a user look's table no longer fail to read. ([#1189](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1189))
- **Presets and exported settings declare the process version RAWmakase renders with**, 15.4 instead of 11.0, so Camera Raw renders them as RAWmakase does. On the test chart's look cases, the gap fell from 0.24–0.66 ΔE00 (up to 2.2 for Profile Amounts above 100%) to about 0.01. ([#1193](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1193), [#1188](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1188))
- **Lossy DNGs open.** These are the DNGs that Adobe DNG Converter writes as lossy (with DNG 1.4 compatibility, or in versions from before JPEG XL) and that Lightroom Classic writes from JPEGs, including many phone DNGs that went through either app. They used to fail with "Unsupported RAW variant: DNG compression 34892". ([#1192](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1192))
- **Another fix to try for the screen flicker on Windows.** While Windows' HDR is off, the window now draws to an ordinary 8-bit surface instead of a 16-bit HDR one, and it switches back when HDR is turned on. We couldn't reproduce the flicker, so if you still see it, please say so in [#1126](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1126). The issue report's system information now includes a `GPU surface` line that will help. ([#1201](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1201))

## Upgrade notes

- **Some saved edits render differently**, closer to Camera Raw: black & white photos with colour grading or a colour look, and Lightroom edits that put a colour look over black and white.
- **Presets saved by earlier releases still name their look without its settings**, so Lightroom still won't render it. Save the preset again with this release.
- Fit Camera Look results already saved in an edit render as before. Fitting a photo again gives the new fit.

## Known limitations

- **Merge to HDR** doesn't yet carry the camera's maker note into the DNG, and has no overlay showing what Deghost took out. With Create Stack, the merge always goes on top of the stack.
- **Import Photos** can't copy to an exFAT drive on macOS or to an SMB network share yet, and refuses those destinations. It doesn't apply metadata or develop presets during import, make a second copy or eject the card.
