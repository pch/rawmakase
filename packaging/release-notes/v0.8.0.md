**TL;DR:** Shadows and Highlights now look very nearly as they do in Lightroom, and Dehaze is much closer too. New: Edit In, Auto Sync, Lights Out, background colors, rotate keys and a JPEG file size limit. Many of these came from [@m-stanczak](https://github.com/m-stanczak).

RAWmakase 0.8.0 brings **Shadows, Highlights and Dehaze much closer to Lightroom**. An edit that pushes these sliders now looks very nearly as it does in Lightroom: deep shadows keep their color, bright skies keep their detail, and strong Dehaze no longer clips or leaves bright halos around edges.

It also adds **Edit In** for opening a photo in Photoshop, Affinity or any other editor, **Auto Sync** and Copy / Paste / Sync buttons in Develop, **Lights Out** on **L**, a choice of **background color** behind the photo, **Cmd+[ / Cmd+]** to rotate photos and **Limit File Size To** for JPEG exports. The zoom now stays put as you move between photos, and the left panel and filmstrip stay hidden or shown in both Library and Develop.

Many of these come from [@m-stanczak](https://github.com/m-stanczak), who sent a whole series of them this release. See [Thanks](#thanks).

## New

- **Edit In.** Open a photo in another program from **Edit In** at the top of its right-click menu, from Develop's photo menu, from **Photo ▸ Edit in External Editor** on macOS, or with **⌘E** (Ctrl+E on Windows and Linux) for the first editor. Add your editors in the new **Preferences ▸ External Editing** tab and choose the color space of the files sent out (ProPhoto RGB, Adobe RGB or sRGB, always 16-bit TIFF). A RAW is sent as `<name>-Edit.tif` with your edit applied, added to the catalog and stacked on the original. For other files you choose Edit Original, Edit a Copy, or Edit a Copy with RAWmakase Adjustments, as in Lightroom. When you save in the other program, RAWmakase picks up the new version in the background and updates its thumbnail and preview. ([#1266](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1266), by [@m-stanczak](https://github.com/m-stanczak), [#1263](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1263))
- **Auto Sync, and Copy / Paste / Sync buttons above the filmstrip.** In Develop, **Copy…**, **Paste** and **Sync…** sit at the left of the bar above the filmstrip. Turn on the **Auto** switch beside Sync, and each change to the open photo also goes to the other selected photos. Only the settings you changed are copied, from the groups chosen last in the Sync dialog, so changing Exposure leaves the others' White Balance alone. Auto and Auto White Balance are measured on each photo. One Undo takes back the change on every photo it reached. Auto Sync is also in Develop's Settings menu and on **Cmd+Option+Shift+A**. ([#1262](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1262), by [@m-stanczak](https://github.com/m-stanczak))
- **Lights Out.** **L** dims everything but the photo, a second press blacks it out, and a third turns the lights back on. **Escape** also turns them back on. Menus, dialogs and messages stay visible, and clicks still work. ([#1257](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1257), by [@m-stanczak](https://github.com/m-stanczak))
- **Choose the background color behind the photo.** Right-click around the photo in Develop or the Library's Loupe and choose Default, White, Light Gray, Medium Gray, Dark Gray, Darker Gray or Black, as in Lightroom Classic. The same choice is in **Preferences ▸ Display ▸ Background ▸ Fill Color**. ([#1251](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1251), [#718](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/718))
- **Rotate photos with Cmd+[ and Cmd+]** (Ctrl+[ and Ctrl+] on Windows and Linux), as in Lightroom. In Develop each press turns the open photo a quarter turn and is one History step. In the Library the keys turn every selected photo in one batch, which one Undo takes back. A photo no longer shows squashed for a moment while its turn renders. Both keys can be rebound in **Preferences ▸ Shortcuts**. ([#1275](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1275), by [@m-stanczak](https://github.com/m-stanczak))
- **Limit File Size To for JPEG exports.** Under Quality in the Export dialog, set a limit in K (1000 bytes). Each photo is then written at the highest quality that fits, metadata and any HDR gain map included. If a photo can't fit even at the lowest quality, it is exported as small as it can be and the export summary says so. ([#1255](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1255), [#1253](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1253))

## Improved

- **Shadows and Highlights now track Lightroom closely.** RAWmakase separates detail from overall brightness in a new way and has been refitted to Camera Raw 18.7, the engine Lightroom uses. Compared with Camera Raw 18.7 on 54 to 57 test photos, the median difference for **Shadows +100** fell from 1.74 to 1.19 ΔE00. Every Highlights setting, from −100 to +100, is now between 0.52 and 0.68 ΔE00, down from 0.58 to 0.86. Under strong Shadows, deep oranges, yellows and greens keep their saturation, and the darkest tones are no longer lifted too far. Under strong Highlights, skies and clouds keep their small-scale contrast and bright colors are less over- or under-saturated. Using Shadows and Highlights together is also closer: on 33 photos, Shadows −100 with Highlights −100 went from 1.25 to 0.86 ΔE00. ([#1267](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1267), [#1274](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1274), [#1281](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1281), [#1133](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1133))
- **Dehaze, positive and negative, is much closer to Lightroom and no longer clips or leaves halos.** Both directions now come from one haze model. On 52 held-out photos compared with Camera Raw 18.7, **Dehaze −100** went from a median of 5.62 to 1.47 ΔE00 and **Dehaze +100** from 2.95 to 2.25, with less clipping at every setting. The bright rims around silhouettes and the dark clipping along edges reported in [#600](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/600) are gone. ([#1282](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1282))
- **The zoom stays as you move between photos.** Zoomed in, the next photo opens zoomed at once, in Develop as in the Loupe. It no longer opens fitted and then jumps, and the zoom slider no longer jumps around while the photo loads. ([#1258](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1258), by [@m-stanczak](https://github.com/m-stanczak))
- **The left panel and filmstrip are shared by Library and Develop.** Hiding either one in one module now hides it in the other too. The right panel is still each module's own. ([#1261](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1261))
- **Develop's toolbar fits narrow windows.** Buttons with no room move to the top of the photo menu (•••), and nothing overlaps at any width. ([#1264](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1264), by [@m-stanczak](https://github.com/m-stanczak))
- **Submenus look like the rest of the menu.** Their arrows line up with the shortcuts and their labels with the other rows. ([#1265](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1265), by [@m-stanczak](https://github.com/m-stanczak))

## Fixed

- **Photos shot at 16:9 in camera no longer flash squeezed** as you move to them with ← / →. ([#1260](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1260), [#1256](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1256))
- **Cancelling an export with AI Denoise stops its denoising too.** Before, every queued AI Denoise job of a cancelled export still ran, keeping the GPU or CPU busy long after the export was gone. ([#1276](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1276))
- **Progress that could stick or vanish.** Reference View no longer shows a spinner for good after its photo is developed again. A preview build's list of failed photos stays until you dismiss it. **Check Now** and update downloads can no longer hang on "Checking…". ([#1277](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1277))
- **AI masks.** A selection that waited for a model download no longer silently disappears. An extension is no longer stopped the moment a long request such as a download ends. Cancelling selections on a slow computer no longer marks the AI masks extension as failed. A remote AI-mask worker keeps working after a model run fails unexpectedly. ([#1280](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1280), [#1283](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1283), [#1278](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1278))
- **Reinstalling Lensfun's lens database after an interrupted removal installs all of it.** Before, some lenses could be missing until it was removed again. ([#1278](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1278))
- **Automation waits for the open photo's edit.** `preview` and `export` from MCP and `rawmakase control` now answer `not_ready`, with the reason, while an edit is still being applied or Auto, Upright or Auto Straighten is still working. Before, they wrote the unfinished edit. A new `state.output_waits` field says when they can go ahead. ([#1279](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1279))
- **A removed photo's id is never given to a new photo**, so a late request from an extension or MCP can't land on the wrong photo. ([#1272](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1272))

## Changed

- **Every photo has a UUID of its own**, which extensions will be able to use to find a photo again later. Each photo gets one the first time this release opens the catalog. ([#1271](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1271))

## Upgrade notes

- **Saved edits that use Shadows, Highlights or Dehaze look different**, closer to Lightroom. Your slider values are kept as they were.
- **After this release opens a catalog, older releases are more limited with it.** Releases before 0.8.0 refuse **Export as Catalog** on it ("A later release of RAWmakase added to this catalog (photo_uuids)"). Releases 0.6.1 to 0.7.2 also stop removing unused masks and AI Remove patches from it. 0.8.0 still removes them.
