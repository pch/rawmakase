RAWmakase 0.7.0 brings **collections** as Lightroom Classic has them, **editable previews** for photos whose RAWs are offline, and **reusable keywords** across folders and catalogs. It also uses much less memory.

Collections, smart collections and collection sets can now be created and edited in the Library, and Lightroom's smart collections import with their rules and update live. Editable previews let you keep editing a photo in Develop while its drive is unplugged. Keywords you've used before are suggested as you type, listed in a Keyword List panel and kept in Keyword Sets. A 61-megapixel photo opens with about 3 GB less at its peak, and an idle Library gives back what it no longer uses on macOS and Linux. Also in this release: HaldCLUT film LUTs as looks, Filter Folders, Shadows and Highlights closer to Camera Raw, cleaner high-ISO shadows and AI Denoise, iPhone and DJI DNG corners, and straightened exports at Lightroom's size.

## New

- **Collections, collection sets and the Quick Collection, as in Lightroom Classic.** ([#998](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/998))
  - **Create Collection…** and **Create Collection Set…** are at the foot of the Collections panel and in each row's right-click menu, with **Rename…** and **Delete…**. Deleting never removes photos from the catalog. Drag collections and sets onto a set to move them. Selecting a set shows the photos of every collection inside it. ([#1145](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1145))
  - Add photos by dragging them onto a collection, or with **Add to Collection** in the thumbnail menu. ([#1145](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1145))
  - **B** adds the selection to the target collection, or takes it out when every photo is already in it. The Quick Collection is the target until you choose **Set as Target Collection** on another. **Save Quick Collection…** (⌘⌥B) saves it as a collection, and ⌘N creates a collection. ([#1152](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1152))
  - A badge in a thumbnail's footer shows that the photo is in a collection. Click it, or use **Go to Collection** in the thumbnail menu, to go there. ([#1152](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1152))
  - Each collection keeps its own sort. In **Custom Order**, drag photos in the grid to rearrange them. ([#1154](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1154))
- **Smart collections.** **New Smart Collection…** (Catalog menu) and **Edit Smart Collection…** open Lightroom's rule editor: match all, any or none of the rules, with nested groups (⌥-click **+**). Members update as you rate, label, keyword or edit photos. Rules are stored as Lightroom stores them. New catalogs start with Lightroom's default smart collections (Colored Red, Five Stars, Past Month, Recently Modified, Video Files and Without Keywords). ([#1144](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1144))
- **Lightroom catalog imports bring smart collections, collection sorts, the target collection and keyword synonyms.** A smart collection whose rules RAWmakase can't evaluate (such as Has Likes, publish services, or fields the catalog doesn't keep, like City) lists no photos and says why, rather than showing a partial match. ([#1140](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1140))
- **Editable previews.** Select photos in the Library and choose **Build Editable Previews** while their RAWs are online. Each builds a lossless copy, 2560 pixels on the long edge (about 16 MB). When the RAW is offline, Develop opens the photo from its copy, labelled **Editable Preview**, and your edit is saved for the original. Once the original is back, the photo renders from it again. A badge marks photos that have one, and **Discard Editable Previews** removes them. Removing a photo deletes its copy, and Export as Catalog takes the copies along. ([#1170](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1170), [#1143](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1143), [#1022](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1022))
- **Reuse keywords across folders and catalogs.** ([#1141](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1141), [#559](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/559))
  - The keyword field suggests matching keywords as you type, shown as `Child < Parent`. ↓/↑ choose one, and Tab or Return applies it.
  - The **Keyword List** panel lists every keyword as a tree with photo counts and a filter. Its boxes add or remove a keyword on the selection, and its arrow shows that keyword's photos. It also creates, renames and deletes keywords, and purges unused ones.
  - **Keyword Sets** give nine buttons, also on ⌥1–⌥9, with **Recent Keywords** and Lightroom's three built-in sets. Your own sets are kept as Lightroom `.lrtemplate` files and work in every catalog. Lightroom Classic's own sets are read but never written.
- **HaldCLUT film LUTs import as looks**, such as RawTherapee's Film Simulation packs. **Import LUTs…** in Basic's profile menu takes `.png` HaldCLUTs beside `.cube` files. When importing a folder, sample images that come with a pack are skipped. ([#1185](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1185), [#668](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/668))
- **Filter Folders.** A search field at the top of the Folders panel shows only folders whose names contain what you type, including folders inside folded ones. Enter opens the first match. ([#1139](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1139), [#1004](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1004))
- **Merge to HDR (incomplete, command line only).** `rawmakase merge-hdr` merges a tripod bracket (2 to 9 RAWs from one camera) into a linear floating-point DNG that opens as an ordinary raw here, in Lightroom and in Camera Raw. Merge to HDR isn't finished: there's no UI for it in the app yet, and it doesn't align or deghost frames. ([#1178](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1178))

## Improved

- **Much less memory.** Measured on an M1 Pro (macOS 27.2) with a release build ([#1174](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1174), [#1179](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1179), [#1181](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1181)):
  - A 61-megapixel photo open at Fit went from 8.7 GB to 2.9 GB, and a 24-megapixel one from 4.0 GB to 1.3 GB.
  - Opening a 61-megapixel photo peaked at 4.6 to 4.8 GB, down from 7.2 to 7.8 GB.
  - The Library, idle after rendering previews for 60 edited RAWs, went from 4.2 GB to 0.29 GB.
  - A photo's images and cached stages are freed when you leave it.
  - On Linux with glibc, the Library, idle after rendering previews for 48 edited RAWs, went from 3.1 GB to 0.81 GB ([#1176](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1176)). Windows wasn't measured.
- **Thumbnails and photo info read only part of each RAW**, on local disks as well as network volumes, and now for RAF and CR2 files too. Reading the photo info of 3,000 new RAWs read 22 GB instead of 133 GB. ([#1174](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1174), [#1177](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1177))
- **Shadows and Highlights are closer to Camera Raw.** Negative Shadows and positive Highlights now adapt to each photo, as the other directions already did. Shadows and Highlights moved together now render as Camera Raw's pair, which differs from their sum by up to half a stop. ([#1128](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1128), [#1153](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1153))
- **High-ISO deep shadows no longer take on a colour cast** that Camera Raw doesn't have, such as magenta on a Nikon Z fc at ISO 204,800. ([#1180](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1180))
- **AI Denoise below Amount 100 no longer brings colour noise back**, and leaves no colour blotches in dark areas at ISO 32,000 and above. ([#1167](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1167))
- **Warm and cool white balances match Camera Raw more closely.** At warm or cool settings, photos came out darker, with their colour shifted too far (1.58 times Camera Raw's shift at 3000 K). RAWmakase Standard, RAWmakase Color and the colour matrix fallback also adapt colours to the chosen white as Camera Raw does. ([#1148](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1148))
- **Select Sky finds the sky between branches and in lattices** such as trusses and fences. ([#1069](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1069), [#801](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/801))
- **The Tone Curve's histogram shows what the curve takes in**, so it stays still while you drag the curve. Each colour curve shows its own channel. ([#1155](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1155))
- **More third-party looks import**, such as Really Nice Images' film profiles. Looks can now carry Whites, Vibrance, Texture, Calibration and Shadows Tint. ([#1161](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1161), [#949](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/949))
- **Lightroom edits from before process version 2012 keep their tone.** Contrast, Brightness, Shadows, Fill Light, Recovery and Clarity are converted as Lightroom converts them, instead of being dropped. ([#1149](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1149))
- **A retouch extension that stops unexpectedly says so** and offers **Extension details…**, which opens its About tab and log. ([#1142](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1142), [#1118](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1118))
- **The sidebars line up with Develop's panels**, and the left panel's toggle no longer moves when you show or hide the sidebar. ([#1163](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1163))

## Fixed

- **iPhone and DJI DNGs no longer have dark corners.** Their vignetting correction was ignored, so corners came out 0.3 to 1.0 stop darker than in Camera Raw. ([#1162](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1162))
- **Straightened exports come out at Lightroom's size.** RAWmakase sized them by the enlarged frame, so a crop straightened by 5° came out 1.11 times larger than Camera Raw's, and by 45° 1.65 times. The whole frame at +45° from Lightroom is no longer refused as an invalid crop. ([#1147](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1147))
- **Lightroom crops that reach past the straightened frame keep their size and place**, instead of coming out smaller. Dragging such a crop's side no longer crashes, and moving a crop no longer resizes it. ([#1146](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1146))
- **HDR merges from Lightroom and HDRMerge keep their deep shadows.** Floating-point DNGs were rounded to 16 bits on opening, which lost shadows in merges of more than about 16 stops. ([#1166](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1166))
- **Canon EOS Rebel T8i photos get their colour matrix**, under the name EOS 850D. Without an installed Adobe profile they opened with no colour matrix. ([#1184](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1184))
- **The Defringe eyedropper no longer covers its heading**, and the checkboxes without a label line up with the panel's headings. ([#1160](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1160), [#1159](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1159))
- **Possible fix for screen flicker on Windows.** RAWmakase read the display's settings every two seconds while it redrew, and now reads them again only when Windows announces a change, or when an HDR-capable display's window comes into focus. We couldn't reproduce the flicker, so please tell us in [#1126](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1126) if you still see it. ([#1157](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1157))
- **A failed AI Denoise model download shows the real error**, not a 404 from a second source that never existed. ([#1165](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1165))

## Upgrade notes

- **Some saved edits render differently**, closer to Camera Raw: Shadows and Highlights (most of all negative Shadows, positive Highlights, or both sliders set), warm and cool white balances, high-ISO shadows, AI Denoise below 100, iPhone and DJI DNGs, and straightened exports, which come out smaller. Library previews of affected photos are rebuilt once.
- **Smart collections in catalogs imported from Lightroom by an earlier release have no rules**, so they list no photos. Import the `.lrcat` again, or give them rules with **Edit Smart Collection…**.
- **After this release opens a catalog, export it as a catalog with this release.** Earlier releases' **Export as Catalog** refuses it because of its new tables. They still open the catalog.
- The Lightroom pre-2012 conversion and the crop fixes apply to Lightroom edits RAWmakase reads from now on. Photos already in your catalog keep the edit they were imported with.

## Known limitations

- Editable previews are built for RAWs only. An offline photo opened from its copy can't be exported, and AI masks, AI Denoise, AI Remove and Fit Camera Look are off. Sync, Paste Settings and presets from the Library still refuse offline photos. Lightroom's Smart Previews aren't imported.
- **Filter Folders** matches what you type as one phrase, while Lightroom matches each word separately.
