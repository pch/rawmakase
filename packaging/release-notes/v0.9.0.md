**TL;DR:** Exports now run on the GPU by default (including HDR masters and single-pass gain maps), Select Sky runs ~6× faster on Apple Silicon's GPU, and AI Denoise has an opt-in Apple Neural Engine mode. New: a map in the Metadata panel, extension export destinations (**Send To**), hold-to-peek on slider labels, a zoom % menu, a toggle for embedded lens corrections, **Delete Permanently** for drives without a Trash, and **Ask before quitting** — plus major Camera Raw 18.7 parity improvements across RAWs and rendered files, and a Windows `dxcompiler.dll` crash fix.

RAWmakase 0.9.0 brings **GPU-accelerated exports** (including HDR masters, single-pass ISO 21496-1 gain-map JPEGs, and shared GPU noise reduction and Texture pyramids), **Apple Silicon Core ML acceleration** for Select Sky and (opt-in) AI Denoise, **export destinations for extensions**, a **Metadata panel map**, and a broad set of **Camera Raw 18.7 color and tone parity improvements** covering Clarity, Whites and Blacks, Saturation and Vibrance, Point and Parametric Curves, Look Profile Amount above 100%, high-ISO shadow neutrality, and rendered files (JPEG, PNG and TIFF).

This release includes contributions from [@ljbergmann](https://github.com/ljbergmann), [@boredmarcin](https://github.com/boredmarcin), [@gradusnikov](https://github.com/gradusnikov) and [@jeremyh](https://github.com/jeremyh). See [Thanks](#thanks).

## New

- **Map in the Metadata panel.** Shows pins for where GPS-tagged photos were taken right under the GPS row, with drag, double-click, pinch and +/− zoom plus **Open in Browser** for OpenStreetMap. Off by default for privacy (fetching tiles tells the tile server roughly where photos were taken); turn it on in **Preferences ▸ General ▸ Map**, where you can also set any HTTPS tile server. ([#1290](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1290), by [@ljbergmann](https://github.com/ljbergmann))
- **Export destinations for extensions (Send To).** Extensions can now declare export destinations with per-export options (manifest `api = 4`). Once an enabled extension offers one, a **Send To** row appears under Post-Processing in the Export dialog along with its options, and **Export with Previous** remembers them. Files are delivered one by one with each photo's UUID and optional catalog metadata, and active deliveries can be stopped from the top bar or the extension's page. ([#1249](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1249), by [@boredmarcin](https://github.com/boredmarcin))
- **AI Denoise on Apple's Neural Engine (opt-in).** On Apple Silicon with macOS 14 or later, turn on **Preferences ▸ Performance ▸ AI Denoise ▸ Use the Neural Engine** to run AI Denoise in half precision via Core ML instead of the GPU. Nothing extra is downloaded: RAWmakase converts the weights locally from the installed ONNX model on first run and caches the compiled model, falling back to GPU and CPU on any error. On an M4 Pro with a 47 MP Leica DNG, a full AI Denoise job dropped from 3.4–3.8 s on the GPU to 1.9–2.1 s on the Neural Engine (91.7 dB vs. CPU; 0.4% of 8-bit values differ by at most 3 levels). ([#1287](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1287), by [@ljbergmann](https://github.com/ljbergmann))
- **Hold a slider's label to peek at its default.** Holding the mouse button down on any slider label temporarily previews that slider at its default value and restores your edit when you release (double-clicking still resets it). ([#1298](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1298), by [@jeremyh](https://github.com/jeremyh))
- **Zoom level menu and remembered zoom level.** Clicking the level beside the zoom slider opens a menu with **Fit** and preset levels from **12.5%** to **1600%**, including levels below Fit. Your last chosen zoom level is remembered between launches (RAWmakase still opens at Fit, and clicking the photo or pressing **Z** zooms to that level). ([#1288](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1288), [#1210](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1210))
- **Switch for lens corrections stored in the file.** For RAWs that carry always-on lens corrections (such as DNG OpcodeList3 warps, vignette and GainMap grids, or manufacturer built-in data), Lens Corrections now shows a **Lens correction stored in the file** switch (on by default) and explains clearly when the file's own correction applies or is replaced by an imported profile or Lensfun lens. ([#1306](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1306), [#1291](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1291), [#1289](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1289))
- **Delete Permanently for photos on drives with no Trash.** When deleting photos on an SMB share or another drive without a Trash, Move to Trash now leaves those files out instead of failing and offers **Delete Permanently…** behind a separate confirmation dialog with a warning. Photos on drives with a Trash are never deleted permanently. ([#1304](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1304), [#1273](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1273))
- **Ask before quitting.** Enable **Preferences ▸ General ▸ Quitting ▸ Ask before quitting** (off by default) to confirm before closing RAWmakase from Cmd+Q / Ctrl+Q, the window close button, or a Dock quit; **Restart to Update** still proceeds without asking. ([#1285](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1285), by [@boredmarcin](https://github.com/boredmarcin))

## Improved

- **GPU-accelerated exports and shared GPU noise reduction & Texture.** Desktop exports now run on a dedicated headless GPU backend by default — covering SDR exports, HDR masters, single-pass HDR+SDR gain-map JPEGs, noise reduction, Texture, sharpening, vignette, grain and Lanczos3 downscaling — with automatic CPU fallback for unsupported recipes or GPU errors (disable with `RAWMAKASE_EXPORT_GPU=0`). Develop previews share the same GPU Laplacian pyramids for noise reduction and Texture. On the command line, `rawmakase render` and `rawmakase render-batch` stay on the CPU by default and accept `--gpu` to opt in. ([#1300](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1300), [#1305](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1305), [#1313](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1313), [#1317](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1317), [#1319](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1319))
- **Select Sky runs on Apple Silicon's GPU.** On Apple Silicon with macOS 15 or later, Select Sky now runs via Core ML on the GPU automatically using weights converted locally from the installed ONNX model (falling back to CPU for uncommon aspect ratios like panoramas or on any error). Measured on 12 photos at 1280 px on an M4 Pro, median selection time dropped from 3.32 s on CPU to 0.50 s on GPU (about 6× faster; median mask IoU 0.9998 across 42 photos). ([#1293](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1293), by [@ljbergmann](https://github.com/ljbergmann))
- **Closer Camera Raw 18.7 parity on RAWs.**
  - **Positive Clarity** moved to a full-depth symmetric local Laplacian rule (in 4-fold cross-validation on 322 real photos, median mean `|ΔL*|` dropped from 2.81 to 1.39 and worst-region error from 9.57 to 4.51; positive HDR Clarity no longer overshoots near white). ([#1286](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1286), [#1297](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1297), [#1133](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1133))
  - **SDR Whites and Blacks** track Camera Raw closely across both directions, including high positive Blacks, negative Whites, dark scenes with small clipped skies or night streetlamps, and `DefaultBlackRender::None` profiles (on 51–55 unseen validation photos, `Blacks +100` median ΔE00 dropped from 1.23 to 0.60 and `Whites −100` from 0.94 to 0.54). ([#1295](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1295), [#1328](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1328), [#1133](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1133))
  - **RAW Saturation and Vibrance** moved to their true pre-tone-curve stages on both CPU and GPU (on 32 validation RAWs, median ΔE00 improved from 0.86 to 0.61 for `Saturation +50`, 1.20 to 0.69 for `Vibrance +100`, and 0.52 to 0.24 for `Vibrance −100`). ([#1325](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1325), [#1329](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1329))
  - **Master Point Curve, Refine Saturation, Parametric Shadows and Look Profile Amount > 100%** now match Camera Raw 18.7 on saturated colors, intermediate Refine Saturation values, deep shadows under `Parametric Shadows −100` (improved on 9/9 test photos, removing a ~4 L* deep-shadow lift), and look profile amounts between 100% and 200%. ([#1321](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1321), [#1322](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1322), [#1333](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1333))
  - **High-ISO deep shadows under positive Exposure** no longer pick up a magenta/blue cast from clipped below-black demosaic noise. ([#1316](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1316), [#1133](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1133))
  - **Cleaner edges under strong Shadows and Highlights.** Per-corner guided upsampling of the local tone base removes dark edge halos around high-contrast silhouettes without introducing bokeh solarization bands. ([#1315](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1315), [#1335](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1335))
- **Closer Camera Raw 18.7 parity on rendered files (JPEG, PNG and TIFF).** Exposure on saturated colors (synthetic `Exposure +2/+3` max ΔE00 cut by 90–94%), Saturation and Vibrance (10 CC0 JPEGs: `Saturation +100` mean ΔE00 down from 2.42 to 0.88), Dehaze, Clarity, extreme Shadows+/Highlights+, and cool-magenta White Balance (plus HDR white balance and strongly blue eyedropper picks) all track Camera Raw 18.7 much more closely on CPU and GPU. ([#1320](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1320), [#1323](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1323), [#1324](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1324), [#1326](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1326), [#1327](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1327))
- **Minimap corner is remembered between launches.** Dragging the zoomed-in minimap (Navigator) to any corner now persists across restarts. ([#1339](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1339), [#1336](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1336))

## Fixed

- **Windows crash from a third-party `dxcompiler.dll` on `PATH`.** Windows packages now bundle Microsoft's official `dxcompiler.dll` (`v1.9.2609`) beside `rawmakase.exe`, stop searching `PATH` for it (which could load an incompatible DLL from another app such as Obsidian and crash with `0xC000001D`), and fall back cleanly to FXC when needed. ([#1334](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1334), [#1254](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1254))
- **RAWs without a usable embedded preview now show a thumbnail in the Library.** Scanner DNGs (such as SilverFast linear DNGs with uncompressed 16-bit greyscale previews) and preview-less RAWs are now developed with Raw Defaults automatically in the background instead of appearing blank until opened in Develop. ([#1312](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1312), by [@gradusnikov](https://github.com/gradusnikov))
- **JPEGs and PNGs saved under a RAW extension now open normally.** Files saved as JPEG or PNG with a `.CR2`, `.NEF`, `.DNG` or other RAW extension (for example from older tools like Picasa) are now detected by content and open with thumbnails, metadata, Develop and export intact, while Raspberry Pi RAWs remain RAWs. ([#1311](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1311), by [@gradusnikov](https://github.com/gradusnikov))
- **Filmstrip photo menu Export… exports the whole selection.** Right-clicking a selected photo in the filmstrip in Develop (or Loupe, Compare and Survey) and choosing **Export…** or **Export with Previous** now exports all selected photos instead of only the clicked photo. ([#1331](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1331))
- **Library thumbnails update after edits made with the Crop tool open.** Rotating, cropping or straightening with Crop & Straighten open now refreshes the filmstrip and Library grid thumbnails without restarting the app. ([#1332](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1332), [#1330](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1330))
- **Zooming in from below Fit** now steps cleanly and stops at Fit instead of jumping straight to or past Fit, and the Library Loupe no longer draws a minimap when a JPEG, TIFF or PNG is zoomed out completely into view. ([#1288](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1288))

## Upgrade notes

- **Saved edits that use Clarity, Whites, Blacks, Saturation, Vibrance, Master/Parametric Curves, Look Profile Amount > 100%, or rendered-file Exposure, White Balance or Dehaze will render differently**, closer to Lightroom / Camera Raw 18.7. Saved slider values are unchanged.
- **Unfinished permanent-delete journals:** if a permanent deletion on a drive without Trash is cut short before completing, releases before 0.9.0 fail closed on that unfinished file-operation entry until it is resolved in 0.9.0.
- **Cached RAW decodes rebuild once** to pick up signed below-black demosaic noise (`rawmakase-raw 5`); catalogs and edits are unaffected.
