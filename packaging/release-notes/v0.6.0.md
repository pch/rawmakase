RAWmakase 0.6.0 brings **AI Retouch**, an experimental tool that removes or replaces what you paint over, through the official [AI Retouch extension](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase-ai-retouch).

Paint over a tourist, a power line or a sign, optionally say what to do ("remove the dogs, keep the people", "a wooden bench"), and the extension regenerates that area. **AI Retouch** sends the area to a hosted model (fal.ai or Replicate) with your own API key. **AI Local Remove** runs a model on your own computer, with no account or upload. The result is saved with your edit as a patch, so the photo renders the same later without the extension or the service. AI Retouch is **experimental**: see [Known limitations](#known-limitations).

Also in this release: Lightroom straightened crops come out the right way, Bayer RAWs open faster, catalogs get much smaller, F11 full screen on Windows and Linux, a redesigned **Preferences ▸ Extensions**, and many more HDR controls that render in HDR.

## New

- **AI Retouch (experimental).** Install it from **Preferences ▸ Extensions ▸ Available**, or with **Add Extension ▸ From GitHub…** and `https://github.com/pch/rawmakase-ai-retouch`. It adds an AI Retouch tool to Develop's rail with two tabs:
  - **AI Retouch** sends the area to fal.ai (the default) or Replicate, with your own account and key. Its **Mode** is **AI Remove**, which fills what you painted with what could be behind it, or **AI Replace**, which fills it with what your **Prompt** describes. Each request is one paid call to the service. The extension's [README](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase-ai-retouch#services-and-models) lists the models and their prices.
  - **AI Local Remove** removes what you paint with LaMa, run by the extension on your CPU. It needs no account or key, and nothing is uploaded. The model (198 MiB) downloads on first use.

  Paint as many strokes as you need, then press **Send** or Enter. Before an extension first uploads anything, RAWmakase asks whether it may send the painted area and its surroundings. Undo and Redo take strokes off and put them back before you send. A result applies only if the photo hasn't changed since it was sent. Otherwise it's dropped and never resent by itself, since each request costs money. The result is one spot in History. It works like a Heal spot whose Feather and Opacity you can change, and you can hide or delete it. RAWmakase renders the area and keeps the colour work. Outside what you painted, the photo keeps its own pixels, and the fill takes on the photo's grain and texture. ([#1093](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1093), [#1110](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1110), [#1111](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1111))
- **Extensions can keep secrets.** API keys and other secret settings are kept in the system's credential store (the macOS keychain, the Windows Credential Manager or Secret Service on Linux), never in RAWmakase's settings files or logs. Uninstalling an extension deletes its keys. Official extensions that RAWmakase offers under **Available** are marked **Official** once installed from their own repository, not **Not reviewed**. ([#1093](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1093), [#1110](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1110))
- **Extensions can add photos as they arrive**, as a watched folder or a tethered camera would. They can do this only after you tick **Add its photos to <catalog>** on the extension's page. What you're editing, typing or haven't saved yet stays as it was. This release only adds the means for extensions: it doesn't ship a watched-folder or tethering extension. ([#1089](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1089))
- **Visualize HDR**, as in Lightroom. A checkbox under the Basic panel's HDR switch colours the photo by how far above SDR white it reaches: cyan up to +1 stop, then blue, purple and magenta. The histogram uses the same colours. It also works on SDR displays. ([#1055](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1055))
- **F11 toggles full screen** on Windows and Linux. On a Mac it has no default key, but you can give it one in **Preferences ▸ Shortcuts**. ([#1107](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1107), [#1096](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1096))

## Improved

- **Bayer RAWs open and export at full size faster**, with exactly the same pixels. Over ten CC0 raw.pixls.us files from 46 to 102 MP, the full decode took 23% less time in all, 22–34% less per Bayer file, on an Apple M5 Pro release build. X-Trans files are about as fast as before. ([#1063](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1063))
- **Catalogs are much smaller.** Every saved edit, History step and snapshot used to carry its own copy of the camera profile (about 290 KB on a real catalog), and of its Lensfun calibration. Each one is now stored once per catalog. Edits still render the same after a profile file is removed or updated, or on another computer. ([#1073](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1073), [#1074](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1074), [#1076](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1076))
- **Preferences ▸ Extensions is redesigned.** Extensions are listed in a table with their version, status and an on/off switch. Each extension has its own page with **Settings**, **Commands** and **About** tabs. **Add Extension** is a menu, and **Uninstall…** asks first. An extension that can't be loaded is listed under **Couldn't load** with the full reason. Automation settings moved here too. ([#1104](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1104))
- **More controls render in HDR.** Before, these fell back to the SDR version:
  - Dehaze down to −50, and Clarity down to −50;
  - the colour mixer's **Luminance** across its whole range;
  - **Defringe**, also with Black & White;
  - **Refine Saturation** from 50%;
  - a point curve whose last point is left of white, unless it's steep;
  - looks with split toning;
  - the 27 HDR camera profiles with sRGB-encoded tables;
  - a mask's **Whites**, **Saturation** up to +50, **Clarity** from 0 to +50 and **Dehaze** from 0 to +100.

  Positive Whites now keeps colours' saturation, and steep point curves are held back far above white, as in Camera Raw. HDR colour grading is stronger above white. ([#1048](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1048), [#1049](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1049), [#1051](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1051), [#1052](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1052), [#1054](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1054), [#1057](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1057), [#1058](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1058), [#1061](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1061), [#1062](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1062), [#1067](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1067), [#1070](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1070), [#1075](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1075), [#1077](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1077), [#1081](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1081), [#1082](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1082), [#1083](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1083), [#1086](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1086), [#1087](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1087), [#1095](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1095), [#1103](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1103))
- **HDR previews and files**:
  - The GPU now renders the HDR preview of HDR files, so Exposure drags no longer wait for the CPU.
  - AI Denoise shows in the HDR preview.
  - HDR files in other primaries or with curved ICC profiles open as HDR edits.
  - HEIC gain maps open as HDR edits on Linux with libheif.

  ([#1050](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1050), [#1053](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1053), [#1090](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1090), [#1094](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1094))
- **HDR exports**:
  - HDR exports can have a watermark, drawn at SDR white.
  - Gain-map JPEGs also carry Adobe's `hdrgm` XMP, for apps that read only that.
  - PQ JPEG XL files record the brightness their content actually reaches, not PQ's 10,000 nits.

  ([#1088](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1088), [#1091](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1091), [#1092](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1092))
- **Positive Blacks matches Camera Raw on dark photos.** Low-key photos used to come out 0.3 to 1.0 EV too light a few stops above black. ([#1068](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1068))
- **Canon EF-S lenses get their Adobe lens profile.** Their names didn't match Adobe's before, so no lens correction was applied. ([#1065](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1065))

## Fixed

- **Photos straightened in Lightroom turn the right way.** RAWmakase turned them the opposite way, so they came out tilted by twice their angle. It also cropped them to the wrong shape. Both XMP sidecars and imported Lightroom catalogs were affected. Edits RAWmakase writes to XMP, in exports and presets, now open straightened and cropped in Lightroom and Camera Raw too. ([#1071](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1071), [#1080](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1080), [#1059](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1059))
- **Older-process edits from Lightroom 5 catalogs keep their Exposure.** Lightroom 5 also stores zeroed 2012 settings in records from before process version 2012. Importing such a record used to set Exposure to 0 and silently drop Contrast, Brightness, Fill Light and Clarity. Exposure is now kept, and the import warns about the settings it can't convert. ([#1064](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1064), [#1060](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1060))
- **Exports to iCloud-synced folders are no longer hidden** in Finder. ([#1085](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1085), [#1084](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1084))
- **The Loupe stays open on the next photo** after you move its photo to the Trash or remove it from the catalog. Before, it went back to the Grid or jumped to an unrelated photo. ([#1106](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1106), [#1105](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/1105))

## Changed

- **The MIDI extension is off by default.** If you've used MIDI with a device, it stays on after the upgrade. Otherwise, turn it on in **Preferences ▸ Extensions ▸ MIDI**. ([#1047](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1047))
- **Catalogs upgrade when they open.** The **Upgrade catalog…** step before Select Subject is gone, since there's nothing left for it to do. ([#1078](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/1078))

## Upgrade notes

- **Opening a catalog in 0.6.0 upgrades it, and earlier releases can't open it afterwards.** RAWmakase first writes a verified backup beside the catalog (`<name>.before-upgrade-backup`) and shows "Upgrading catalog: n / N edits". If the upgrade fails, the catalog is left unchanged. To go back to 0.5.x, open the backup. Edited previews are rebuilt once after the upgrade.
- An edit with an AI Retouch spot is saved in a newer format, which earlier releases can't read, including in presets and sidecars.
- Edits already in your catalog keep their crop and angle. The Lightroom straighten fix applies to Lightroom edits RAWmakase reads from now on.

## Known limitations

AI Retouch:

- **It's experimental.** Check each result, and undo it if the model changed what it should have kept. A prompt such as "keep the people" works only as well as the model follows it.
- **The AI Retouch tab sends the painted area and its surroundings (up to 2048 pixels a side) to the service you choose, and each request is billed to your account there.** AI Local Remove uploads nothing.
- It works on catalog photos only, once they've fully developed. HDR files and HDR edits are refused for now.
- AI Local Remove has no prompt and works at 512 pixels, so a large area loses detail. It does worse than the hosted model where a person or structure crosses a sharp edge.
- A generated spot can't be moved or resized, and Copy and Sync don't take it to other photos.

HDR still doesn't render **Highlights**, **Whites** above +50, **Clarity** beyond ±50, **Dehaze** below −50, **Refine Saturation** below 50%, steep point curves that end left of white, a darkening vignette's **Highlights**, a mask's **Blacks**, **Hue** or **Highlights**, a mask's **Saturation** above +50, **Clarity** outside 0 to +50 or negative **Dehaze**, or looks that set one of these. Such an edit keeps HDR on, shows its SDR version and says which control is the cause. The other HDR limitations listed for [0.5.0](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/releases/tag/v0.5.0#known-limitations) still apply, except those fixed above.
