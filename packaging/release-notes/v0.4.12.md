RAWmakase 0.4.12 exports in Adobe RGB (1998) and ProPhoto RGB, opens HEIC and HEIF photos on macOS, Windows and Linux, and builds Library previews much faster, including from network volumes. The Crop tool gains Lightroom's padlock and Alt-drag from the centre, and pixel-shift Pentax files open faster. Edits are no longer locked when only a photo's modification time changes, Windows keeps two disks given the same drive letter apart, and the MCP editing tools work with Claude.

## New

- **Export in Adobe RGB (1998) and ProPhoto RGB.** **Export ▸ File Settings ▸ Color Space** offers sRGB (still the default), Adobe RGB (1998) and ProPhoto RGB, with wide-gamut pixels and a matching embedded profile. ProPhoto RGB is offered for 16-bit TIFF only. **Export with Previous** uses the saved choice. `rawmakase render` and `render-batch` take `--color-space srgb|adobe-rgb|prophoto`, and the control socket, `rawmakase control export` and MCP's `export_photo` take an optional `color_space`. The profiles are CC0 (Compact-ICC-Profiles); no Adobe installation or download is needed. ([#903](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/903), [#604](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/604))
- **HEIC and HEIF photos.** HEIC, HEIF and HIF files are added with their folders, and their EXIF, XMP and capture times are read. macOS decodes them with ImageIO. Windows uses the Windows Imaging Component, which needs Microsoft's HEIF Image Extensions and HEVC Video Extensions; RAWmakase says so when they are missing. Linux uses the system's libheif with its HEVC decoder; without it, HEIC photos are listed and opening one says what to install. A Library preview that fails to load is now reported once in the top bar instead of being drawn over the photo. ([#805](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/805), [#800](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/800))
- **The Crop padlock.** A padlock beside **Aspect** shows whether the crop's ratio is locked. Click it or press **A**, as in Lightroom, to lock the crop at its current ratio or unlock it. While the crop is unlocked, the menu names its ratio. ([#896](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/896))
- **Alt-drag resizes the crop from its centre.** Hold Alt (Option on a Mac) while dragging any crop handle to move the opposite side too, keeping the centre in place. This respects a locked ratio, and you can press or release Alt during the drag. ([#899](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/899))
- **Original filename token.** **Rename Photos** and export file naming can use the photo's file name from when it entered the catalogue. For photos added before this release, the token gives the current name. Lightroom catalogue imports carry over Lightroom's original file name. ([#902](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/902))

## Improved

- **Library previews build much faster.** Embedded thumbnails are made by several workers at once. While the Library is in front, edited previews render up to four at a time, using Develop's fast Fit path, and up to 500 photos past the visible ones are rendered ahead. On an 18-core Apple M5 Pro with 24 MP Nikon Z 6 files, 609 embedded thumbnails took 9.7 s instead of 24.3 s, and 24 visible edited previews took 4.6 s instead of 43.3 s. Adding and synchronizing folders reads new files on up to four threads. ([#780](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/780))
- **Library thumbnails load faster from network volumes.** On a network volume, a RAW's Library thumbnail is made from its embedded preview, read on its own, instead of reading the whole file. This covers DNG, RW2, ARW, NEF, CR3, compressed ORF and Huffman PEF files, the formats checked to give the same preview this way. Other formats, and previews smaller than 640 px, still read the whole file. ([#901](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/901), [#883](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/883))
- **Pixel-shift Pentax files open faster.** Opening a pixel-shift PEF from a K-1, K-1 Mark II or K-3 Mark III decodes only the first frame's data. On a busy 10-core M1 Pro this made decoding 3–4× faster; other Pentax PEFs no longer decode the full-size JPEG after their data, which cost about 10%. Decoded samples are unchanged. ([#900](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/900), [#725](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/725))
- **Smoother panning on Linux.** Dragging the navigator glides the photo instead of moving it in leaps. A zoomed-in photo can be dragged without judder, because the sharp view is rendered with a margin around what's visible and kept on whole screen pixels. On slower GPUs, sharp pixels now appear during a pan instead of only after it. ([#890](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/890), [#897](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/897))
- **Library previews show AI Denoise.** Once a photo's AI Denoise result is finished, its Library thumbnail and Loupe preview use it. Before then they show the photo without it, never a partial result. ([#846](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/846))
- **Faster command-line AI Denoise.** `rawmakase render-batch` loads the AI Denoise model once per command instead of once per job. `render`, `render-batch` and `denoise` use the AI Denoise cache folder chosen in Preferences. ([#827](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/827))

## Fixed

- **Edits can be saved after a photo's modification time changes.** If a file's modification time changed but its content didn't (a cloud mount rounding times, a copy that didn't keep them, `touch`), every later save failed with "catalog edit protected", and exporting it from Develop failed the same way. Edits are now tied to the file's size and the start of its content, not to its time. ([#893](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/893), [#870](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/870))
- **A Free crop stays unlocked.** After choosing **Free** and dragging, reopening Crop, moving to another photo, reloading or relaunching no longer locks the crop to a Custom ratio. ([#896](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/896))
- **Windows keeps two disks given the same drive letter apart.** When one disk is unplugged from a letter and another takes it, the Folders panel shows them as two drives, named as Explorer names them, such as `Holiday (F:)`. Each is shown as attached only while that disk is at its letter. ([#895](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/895), [#891](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/891))
- **MCP editing tools work with Claude.** Every editing tool of `rawmakase mcp` failed with `expected struct McpTarget`, because Claude sent the target as a string. The tool schemas now describe it as an object. ([#892](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/892), [#881](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/881))
- **A file replaced while its photo is open is no longer mixed up with it.** RAWmakase no longer saves the old photo's edit onto the new file. It no longer shows or caches the new file's full-size image as the photo that was opened; instead, opening fails with "The file changed while it was opening; open it again." Develop's thumbnail is no longer cached as the new file's. ([#814](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/814), [#831](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/831), [#837](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/837))
- **Auto CA is measured on the photo itself.** Running Auto Tone before any render measured lateral chromatic aberration on a reduced copy. A full-size export served from the decode cache used the half-size image's measurement. ([#869](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/869), [#868](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/868))
- **AI Denoise no longer hangs.** A failure while delivering one result no longer leaves the jobs queued behind it, their exports and Develop waiting forever. Asking for AI Denoise no longer freezes the window when the photo's drive stalls. ([#826](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/826), [#825](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/825))
- **Preferences survive switching releases.** Preferences written by a newer release are kept when this one saves the session, and one unreadable setting resets only itself, not every preference. ([#818](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/818))
- **Removing a virtual copy that is still loading** no longer lets it reappear in the editor. ([#822](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/822))
- **TIFF exports keep the type of a camera's EXIF fields**, such as ExifVersion, instead of writing them as bytes. ([#903](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/903))

## Changed

- **`rawmakase render` starts where the app would.** With `--catalog`, it renders the photo's catalogue edit, as Export does: its saved edit, else its Lightroom edit, else the raw defaults. Without a catalogue, it uses a legacy sidecar as before, else the raw defaults chosen in Preferences instead of Adobe Default. `--recipe` and the adjustment flags still come first. ([#875](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/875))

## Upgrade notes

- Edited Library previews are rendered again once after upgrading, because the way they're made changed.
- `drives.json` gains a disk identity on Windows. Earlier releases still read it. A drive saved by an earlier release is matched by its letter until one of its folders is found again.

## Known limitations

- The preview, histogram and clipping warnings stay sRGB; they don't soft-proof a wide-gamut export.
- Edited Library thumbnails use Develop's Fit render, which can differ slightly from a full render in fine detail and local contrast. Develop's 100% view and exports are unchanged.
- An in-place change that keeps a photo's size and the first 64 KB of its file keeps its edit.
- When the same folder path exists on two Windows disks given the same letter (two camera cards with `F:\DCIM\100CANON`), that catalogue folder still follows whichever disk is attached.
